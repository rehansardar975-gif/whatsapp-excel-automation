01 · The business problem

Contact lists live in Excel. Sending a WhatsApp campaign from them is slow and risky.

  • Numbers typed every way: 050…, +971…, 00971…, two in one cell
  • The same person appears several times
  • No clear record of who agreed to be contacted, or who said STOP
  • The same message copied and pasted hundreds of times
  • No record afterwards of who got it, who didn't, and why
The goal

“I upload my contacts, prepare my message, start, and clearly see what happened.”

The risk with manual work isn't only the time. Messaging people who opted out can get a WhatsApp business number restricted.
